Recommendations & Action Items

Strategies based on this week's actual data

1. Reduce Client Hang-Ups (41.3% of all calls — #1 issue)

With 16,704 hang-ups out of 40K+ calls, this is the single biggest leak. Train agents on a stronger opening hook within the first 5 seconds. Role-play scenarios where clients immediately want to hang up. Consider A/B testing different opening scripts.

2. Convert Answering Machines (29.6%) into Callbacks

11,513 calls hit answering machines. Implement a systematic callback strategy — schedule callbacks during peak hours (10 AM–12 PM, 2–4 PM) when people are more likely home. Track callback-to-transfer conversion rate.

3. Address Dead Air (14.3%) — Potential System Issue

5,806 dead air calls suggest either dialing issues, system lag, or agents not engaging quickly. Investigate if this is a technical problem (auto-dialer timing) or a behavioral one (hesitation to speak first).

4. Coaching for Bottom 6 Agents

Lovel Jane Baros (26), Donita Conde (27), Jolever Cabrera (30), Jovelyn Cortes (30), Jonathan Mario (35), Mary Napigquit (37) — Pair each with a top performer for side-by-side shadowing sessions.

5. Replicate Top Performer Patterns

Aubrey Rose Javier consistently leads (10-16 transfers/day). Record and analyze her calls. What is her opening? How does she handle "not interested"? Create a playbook from her approach.

6. Set Micro-Goals & Real-Time Leaderboard

Instead of just a daily goal of 10, set hourly micro-targets (2/hour). Display a real-time leaderboard visible to all agents — competition drives performance. Celebrate small wins publicly in team chat.
